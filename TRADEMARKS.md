## Project name and branding

The following identify official aurelianOS software and project materials:

- aurelianOS
- AurelianOS
- the official aurelianOS logo
- official aurelianOS product and release names
- other branding specifically identified as official aurelianOS branding

These identifiers remain reserved to the project and their respective rights
holders.

## Forks and modified versions

The Apache License permits users to modify and redistribute aurelianOS.

Forks, derivatives, and modified distributions are allowed, but they must not
use aurelianOS branding in a way that falsely suggests that the modified
software is an official aurelianOS release or that it is endorsed, maintained,
or published by the aurelianOS project.

For example, a fork may describe itself as:

> ExampleOS — based on aurelianOS

or:

> ExampleOS — a fork of aurelianOS

provided the statement is accurate and does not imply official affiliation.

A modified distribution should not present itself as:

> Official aurelianOS

or otherwise use the aurelianOS identity in a manner likely to confuse users
about its origin or endorsement.

## Use of the aurelianOS name

Factual references to aurelianOS are permitted. For example, a project may
state that it is based on, derived from, or forked from aurelianOS when that
statement is accurate.

Use of aurelianOS branding beyond factual reference requires permission from
the applicable rights holder.

## Logos and artwork

Official aurelianOS logos, icons, artwork, and other identifying graphics
may not be used in a modified distribution in a manner that implies that the
distribution is an official aurelianOS product or release.

A fork may replace the official branding with its own.

## No trademark license

Nothing in the Apache License, the aurelianOS source code, this repository,
or this policy grants a trademark license to use aurelianOS branding except
for ordinary factual references.

## Enforcement

This policy represents the project's intended branding policy. Trademark
rights and enforcement requirements vary by jurisdiction.

Nothing in this document is intended to constitute legal advice.
